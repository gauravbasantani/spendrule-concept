# SpendRule concept

An unofficial design concept by Gaurav Basantani ([gauravbasantani.com](https://gauravbasantani.com)): an invoice review for health-system accounts-payable teams where each flagged invoice line is traced to its contract clause, the reviewer approves, disputes or holds it, and only clean lines go to payment. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpendRule. The health system, vendor, contract and numbers in it are made up, and it is not financial or legal advice. Live page: https://gauravbasantani.github.io/spendrule-concept/

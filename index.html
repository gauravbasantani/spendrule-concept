Design concept · Invoice review

Pay only what the contract says

An invoice review for health-system AP teams, built on SpendRule's idea: check every invoice line against the contract before payment.

Gaurav Basantani
gauravbasantani.com
1The problem

Health-system AP teams pay thousands of vendor invoice lines, and a wrong rate or a missing discount looks fine on an invoice.

2The idea

Trace each invoice line to the contract, so the reviewer sees the clause right beside the price.

3What changes

The reviewer approves, disputes or holds each flagged line, and only clean lines go to payment.
